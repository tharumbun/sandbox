This blog is one of Cambodia’s longest-running personal blogs, offering a unique and insightful perspective on technology, culture, and life in the Kingdom. Originally launched in the early 2000s, the blog began as a simple personal journal hosted on Blogger. As the digital landscape evolved, so did the blog’s platform and purpose.

In its second phase, Tharum migrated his blog to WordPress, gaining more flexibility in design and content management. During this period, the blog became a go-to source for thoughtful commentary on Cambodia’s emerging tech scene, media trends, and digital transformation. It served not just as a personal outlet but also as a space for sharing interviews, essays, and reflections on the changing world around him.

Today, this blog runs on **Astro**, a modern web framework that offers speed, simplicity, and a focus on performance. The move to Astro reflects Tharum’s continued passion for staying ahead of the curve in web development and content publishing. The current iteration of the blog is fast, lightweight, and focused on delivering a seamless reading experience—whether you're revisiting past entries or exploring new posts.

From Blogger to WordPress to Astro, the blog’s evolution mirrors Tharum’s journey as a writer, technologist, and digital storyteller. Through it all, the blog has remained a consistent voice in the Cambodian digital sphere—personal, reflective, and always forward-looking.

Visit the blog at [tharum.com](https://tharum.com).
