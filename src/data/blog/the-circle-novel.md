The Circle is a dystopian novel by Dave Eggers, first published in 2013. It explores the dark side of technology, surveillance, and the pursuit of total transparency in the digital age.

The novel’s core ideas are about visibility, validation, and surveillance, which mirror the dynamics that define influencer culture in the 2020s.

Eggers views influencers — or anyone seeking visibility in a networked world — as standing at a crossroads:
• One path leads to empowerment and community.
• The other leads to conformity, surveillance, and loss of self.

In The Circle, influence is both light and shadow — a mirror of our modern digital condition.
